Design prototypes

Five layouts of the same calculator, ordered from least to most on screen. They share one comparison, so changes carry between them.

  1. Sentence One activity stated in a sentence. Every other activity is a multiple of it. Underlined words are the controls. Fraunces, Hanken Grotesk
  2. Bars A ranked list with one bar and one figure per activity. A row opens to its schedule, alternative, other measures and sources. Schibsted Grotesk
  3. True scale Squares whose area matches each total. Anything too small to see is redrawn in an enlarged frame. A square opens its details. Bricolage Grotesque
  4. Scale A ladder where each step is ten times the one below. Your activities are labelled; common activities are ticks you can add. Instrument Sans
  5. Ledger One table with every measure as a column, quantities edited in place, alternatives as sub-rows and totals underneath. IBM Plex Sans Condensed

Not wired in the prototypes: the comparison question, dictation, sharing, the factor library and labour evidence.
